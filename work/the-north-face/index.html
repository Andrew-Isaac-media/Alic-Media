The North Face

A creative collaboration between ALIC co-founder Andrew Iwanicki and The North Face, this winter-focused film was developed through a personal creator partnership connected to the brand's holiday-season messaging. The creative direction was built around a simple idea: winter exploration is compelling because the conditions are real. Cold temperatures, unpredictable weather, and demanding terrain provide the atmosphere for a story in which outerwear is part of the experience rather than the subject of a conventional product demonstration.

The resulting short-form piece combines outdoor cinematography with an experiential style of storytelling. Landscape, movement, and changing conditions establish the setting, while the apparel is integrated into the journey itself. The goal was to communicate the spirit of winter adventure in a way that feels immediate and lived-in, giving viewers a clear sense of the environment and the role of dependable gear within it. This approach reflects a particular strength of founder-led production: developing a concept that connects a product to the experience and audience it was made for.
