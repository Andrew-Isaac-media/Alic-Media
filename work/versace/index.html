Versace

A creative collaboration between ALIC co-founder Andrew Iwanicki and Versace, this fragrance film was developed with a creative direction that explores a less expected side of a luxury product. The original direction emphasized the fragrance's wilder, more adventurous character. Instead of presenting cologne solely as a polished object in a controlled studio environment, the piece places the product within a lifestyle narrative shaped by movement, travel, and the feeling of being out in the world.

The creative challenge was to preserve a sense of refinement while introducing energy and freedom into the visual language. Framing, atmosphere, pacing, and the surrounding environment work together to connect the fragrance with a particular feeling rather than simply listing its qualities. That combination of aspiration and approachability is what makes lifestyle-led product storytelling useful: it helps an audience imagine how a product belongs in their own experiences, while still keeping its identity and presentation intentional.
