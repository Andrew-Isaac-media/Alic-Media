Common questions we get from brands

How much do you guys charge?

We charge according to the scope of your project and needs. Our monthly packages normally sit in the $2,800 to $3,500 range, but we like to iron out those details once we understand the full project.

What does the first meeting look like?

We want to get to know you and your brand. Through asking the right questions, we develop a plan curated to your needs.

How long does it typically take for deliverables to get back to us?

Our turnaround time is seven days. If we have a shoot day on Monday, you can expect the deliverables for that shoot the following Monday.

Why should I hire you guys instead of paying someone with a camera I know for less money?

We want to give you peace of mind and consistent results. Who you decide to trust with this part of your business could drastically impact your growth and future. Why not be absolutely sure of the results instead of taking a shortcut?

How much time am I going to have to spend managing this part of my business if we hire you?

All we ask is a monthly strategy meeting to review results, receive feedback, and schedule the monthly shoot days. Beyond that, you can sit back and watch us grow your business for you.
