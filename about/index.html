The Story Behind ALIC

Built by creators. For brands.

ALIC started with two friends and a shared passion for creating things that mean something. Through years of photography, filmmaking, and working with brands, we realized something: great content isn't just about making something look good. It's about understanding what makes a business different and finding a way to show that to the world. That's why we started ALIC Media. We wanted to build a creative company that works alongside brands, understands their goals, and takes care of the entire production process. We're not here to simply deliver a few videos and disappear. We're here to build lasting relationships and help brands create work they're proud to put their name on.
